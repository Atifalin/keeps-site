Three steps. One drawer of paper, gone.

Keeps is built around one idea: the moment a document enters your home, it should stop being your job to remember it.

Scan anything

Point the camera — VisionKit edge-detection captures clean, straight pages, even multi-page PDFs. Or share a photo or file straight into Keeps.

It understands it

On-device Apple Intelligence reads the page, knows an Aadhaar from a PUC, and pulls the fields that matter — names, numbers, dates. You confirm with one look.

It remembers, so you don't

Expiries become timeline events and reminder ladders — 60 days out, 30, 7, the day of. Birthdays roll forward every year.

Built like an archive, not a folder.

Every detail in Keeps exists to make a document easy to store, easy to trust, and easy to find — for the whole family.

Private, the way a vault should be.

Keeps was built so that we — and Apple, and everyone else — can't read what's inside it. This isn't a policy footnote; it's the architecture.

On your iPhone

Scans, OCR, and fields live in a protected local vault. If you enable sync, it flows through your own private iCloud database — encrypted, invisible to us.

On-device intelligence

Apple Intelligence reads and answers on the phone itself. For harder questions, Private Cloud Compute is opt-in, stateless, and never stored.

Nothing for us

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no servers that receive your data. Face ID guards the door; a masked value needs a second unlock.

Questions, answered.

What does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The on-device AI features need an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone (15 Pro or later); everything else works everywhere.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Processing is on-device. If you opt into Private Cloud Compute for Ask Keeps, requests go through Apple's stateless PCC infrastructure — cryptographically attested, never stored, never used for training.

What happens if I lose my phone?

The vault is locked behind Face ID. With iCloud sync on, your document records restore on a new iPhone signed into your Apple ID. Scanned page images restore via your encrypted iCloud Backup.

Can it read non-English documents?

Yes — Vision OCR handles Devanagari and other Indian scripts, and extraction works across languages.

How much will it cost?

The beta is free. Pricing, if any, will be announced before the App Store release — testers get early notice.

Is there an Android version?

Keeps is deliberately Apple-native — Face ID, iCloud, Siri, Spotlight, Apple Intelligence. No Android plans right now.
